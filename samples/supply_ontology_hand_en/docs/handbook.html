Supply Chain Ontology Sample

This sample demonstrates one-shot knowledge-network import, data binding, metrics, Skills, functions, Actions, and Agent verification.

Core business question

Determine whether product U00-000080 can be delivered by a specified deadline and provide inventory, BOM, production-capacity, and purchasing evidence.

Two operating modes

  • Agent mode: use natural language to trigger retrieval, calculation, and controlled actions.
  • Manual mode: run import, binding, queries, and verification explicitly through CLI/API.

Verification boundary

Forecast cases use 2026-08-15 as the benchmark-as-of date; the historical case is regression-only. Actions default to dry-run and require human confirmation.
